# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Casual visitors who land on totornot.com for a laugh: friends sharing a link, bored scrollers, potato enthusiasts. No accounts, no onboarding; their context is "I have 30 seconds, entertain me." The job to be done is instant fun: tap the better spud, watch the rankings shift, maybe dig into Spud Match to find their personal potato soulmate.

## Product Purpose

Tot or Not is a side-by-side potato dish ranking app. Two dishes enter, one dish leaves. Votes feed a global Elo ranking (K=32); Spud Match runs TOPSIS preference matching across 8 flavor/texture criteria. Success looks like: visitors smile, cast a handful of votes, check the leaderboard, and send the link to someone else. It is a toy built with real engineering, and the design should carry that same wink: serious craft in service of something gleefully unserious.

## Brand Personality

Playful, polished, a little absurd. The humor comes from treating potato dishes with championship-bracket gravity. Voice is deadpan-fun, never childish or sloppy. The interface itself should spark joy; design IS the product here, the UX flows (vote, leaderboard, match) merely host it.

Note: the current retro-diner execution (cream/turquoise/red, neon, checkerboard) is **up for grabs**. A full visual glow-up is planned; UX flows are kept, the visual identity is open to replacement.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS landing**: Inter-on-white, gradient buttons, identical card grids, hero metrics. The default AI/startup look.
- **Kitschy theme overload**: wall-to-wall retro gimmicks (checkerboards, neon, chrome everywhere) drowning the content.
- **The current design specifically**: the light and dark themes are jarring and disjoint, two different designs rather than one design under two lights. Whatever replaces it must unify them.

## Design Principles

1. **One design, two lights.** Light and dark mode are the same design under different illumination: same patterns, same hierarchy, same personality. Never two separate aesthetics.
2. **Joy through craft, not props.** Personality comes from typography, motion, color confidence, and copy, not from piling on theme decorations.
3. **The matchup is the stage.** The head-to-head vote is the hero moment of the product; every screen supports the drama of dish vs. dish.
4. **Deadpan gravity.** Treat potatoes like a world championship. The contrast between polish and absurdity IS the joke; never undercut it with clip-art silliness.
5. **Zero friction stays sacred.** No accounts, no walls, instant play. Design must never add steps to the core loop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, in both themes.
- Full keyboard operability for voting, leaderboard, and Spud Match sliders.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation (the vote slide/winner sequence especially).
- Storybook a11y addon is installed; new components should pass it.
